Results: every version, every file

RSM and ANN analysis of phosphate adsorption capacity (qe, mg/g) for 5 biochar materials: 32 runs × 3 replicates each, 480 lab measurements, none altered or synthesised.

Headline findings

  • Best material: BC:MgCO₃ 1:1 (ANOVA + Tukey, p < 0.001). Plain biochar holds almost nothing.
  • Best conditions (pH limited to 4–8): 65 ppm, 190 min, pH 8, 10 mg dose.
  • Expected qe there: roughly 70–95 mg/g. Highest measured: 91.5 mg/g.
  • Final model (v3) predicts unseen conditions with R² 0.90–0.94 and an average error of about 4 mg/g.
